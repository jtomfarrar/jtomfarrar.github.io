Upper-ocean science at mission scale.

This concept assumes the strongest story is not "about Tom" in isolation. It is the set of programs he leads or helps lead. The site becomes a front door to campaign-scale science, instruments, field operations, and high-resolution ocean observing systems.

Program stack

The existing site has these elements, but they are visually fragmented. Here they are promoted to the primary architecture so visitors can orient around the actual engines of the research.

Primary modules

S-MODE

A coordinated submesoscale experiment combining aircraft, ships, and dozens of autonomous platforms.

  • Best for showing leadership, complexity, and modern field operations.
  • Stronger homepage anchor than a generic research-interest list.

SWOT

Connects the site to a globally recognizable mission and gives satellite validation work a clear home.

  • Raises perceived scale and visibility of the portfolio.
  • Supports clearer narrative for collaborators and media.

Upper Ocean Processes

Provides continuity, instrumentation depth, and a bridge from campaign science to long-duration observing systems.

  • Useful for recruiting engineers, postdocs, and interdisciplinary partners.
  • Helps integrate buoy and vehicle work into one visible system.

Observing systems

Instead of separating research by medium and page type, this concept uses one systems layer to show how satellites, moorings, ships, and uncrewed vehicles contribute to the same scientific questions.

Cross-platform logic

What this structure improves

  • Clarifies why buoys, field campaigns, and satellites all belong on the same homepage.
  • Reduces the current card-by-card document feel.
  • Creates better room for metrics, maps, or selected papers later.
  • Makes the site feel active and operational rather than archival.

Campaign evidence

Strong campaign imagery works as proof that the programs are real, current, and technically demanding. This layout preserves that energy without turning the homepage into a generic photo gallery.

Best use case
